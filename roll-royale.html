    Roll Royale

    Play with friends on one device, or choose vs Bot to play the computer.

    How to play

    Take turns rolling the die and race from the start to square 100.

    Land on a ladder's bottom to climb up. Land on a snake's head to slide down.

    You need the exact number to land on 100. If your roll is too high, you stay put.
